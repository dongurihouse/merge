# Snowy Village Festival Mock Design

## Goal

Regenerate `snowy_village.png` as a stronger pond-centered Christmas festival scene. The revision must preserve the established colorful paper-cut picture-book style, portrait composition, camera angle, and cozy winter palette while fixing the awkward foreground shop placement and activating the frozen pond.

## Output

- Create one non-destructive review image named `snowy_village_festival_v2.png` beside the source mock.
- Keep the source canvas at 941 x 1672 pixels.
- Do not overwrite `snowy_village.png`.

## Composition

Use a festival-crescent layout around the frozen pond:

1. Keep the two log cabins on the far shore, with the smaller cabin on the left and the larger cabin on the right.
2. Place one prominent decorated Christmas tree on the snowy far shore between the cabins. Ground its trunk and gifts naturally in the snow; it must not float on the ice.
3. Relocate the gift stall from the lower-right foreground to the upper-right shoreline beside and slightly below the large cabin. Make it smaller than the cabin so it reads as supporting scenery rather than a competing focal point.
4. Keep the frozen pond as the largest central shape and leave most of its center visually open.
5. Place two translucent ice sculptures on the pond, one toward each side of the upper-middle ice. Use simple festive silhouettes such as a swan and a reindeer. They must read as ice, not opaque stone.
6. Place one small red wooden sled and a pair of skates on the lower portion of the frozen pond. These are secondary details and must not block the pond.
7. Keep the snowman pair on the lower-left bank and the warm lantern rhythm along the right shore.
8. Remove the oversized foreground sled and the oversized lower-right stall from their current positions.

## Visual Hierarchy

1. Frozen pond and decorated Christmas tree.
2. Cabins and ice sculptures.
3. Relocated stall, snowmen, sled, skates, and lanterns.
4. Pines, hills, snowfall, shrubs, and clouds.

The eye should move from the tree down across the ice and then around the crescent of shoreline activities. The scene must remain airy rather than becoming a dense object collage.

## Style Invariants

- Match the source image's high-contrast, colorful, handcrafted paper-cut picture-book rendering.
- Preserve the high three-quarter camera angle and portrait framing.
- Preserve the blue, cream, warm amber, evergreen, red, and lavender palette.
- Preserve softly rounded shapes, visible paper texture, warm window glow, and gentle short contact shadows.
- Keep the existing pond footprint and general shoreline geometry recognizable.
- No people, UI, labels, signage, logos, text, border, or watermark.
- Avoid photorealism, dark cinematic lighting, heavy cast shadows, plastic 3D rendering, or objects clipped by the canvas.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The shop stall is visibly grounded on the upper-right shore and no longer crowds the foreground.
- The pond remains the dominant readable shape.
- The Christmas tree is the main festive focal point without obscuring either cabin.
- Ice sculptures are clearly translucent and sit naturally on the ice.
- The sled and skates sit on the pond and remain small secondary details.
- The result feels like one coherent illustrated scene, with consistent scale, perspective, shadows, and texture.
- The output is exactly 941 x 1672 pixels and saved non-destructively.
