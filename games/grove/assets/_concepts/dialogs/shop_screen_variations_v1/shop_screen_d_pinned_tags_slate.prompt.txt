Design direction D — PINNED TAGS ON A SLATE BOARD. Invert the screen's value structure. Today the
background is cream and the cards are pale, so nothing separates. Here the whole screen is a DEEP muted
slate-blue paper board and every offer is a light warm-cream paper tag pinned onto it, so the cream tags
and golden acorns pop off a quiet dark field.

- The board fills the canvas: deep slate-blue paper mixed toward receding blue #8296AF so it stays a quiet
  background of roughly 32-40% saturation, never a saturated teal. Its surface shows clear paper tooth and
  one broad, very soft upper-left lightening. A slender darker slate frame runs around the board's edge.
- "SHOP" is cut from warm-cream paper straight onto the board at the top, with a short tinted shadow so the
  letters read as paper lifted off the board. The coral close button is a round paper disc pinned at the
  upper right.
- Every offer is a warm-cream paper TAG with a punched round hole at its top, a short length of pale twine
  through the hole, and a small round coral pin head holding the twine to the board. Each tag is rotated
  only two to four degrees, alternating direction between neighbours, so nothing sits on a strict grid and
  the wall reads as hand-pinned. Each tag casts a short soft tinted shadow onto the board, and the twine
  casts a hairline shadow of its own.
- Section headings are torn cream banner strips with frayed, uneven ends, laid flat across the board with
  their words cut into them in ink: "FREE REFILL", "QUICK HELP", "ACORN POUCHES". They are wider than the
  tags beneath them and are pinned at both ends.
- The FREE REFILL tag is the widest single tag on the board, hung on a pale-meadow twine, carrying the
  watering can, "100", and the action-green "FREE" button. The QUICK HELP tag is narrower, carrying the coin
  pouch, "150", and the acorn "5" button.
- The six acorn tags are laid out as three rows of two, and their SIZE grows with their tier: the "80" tag
  is small, and each later tag is a little wider and taller, so the bottom-right "13,000" tag is the largest
  paper on the board. That tag alone hangs on a reward-gold cord instead of twine and carries the gold
  "BEST VALUE" seal pressed over its punched hole. Each tag holds its container, its amount in large ink,
  and its price in an action-green paper button at the tag's foot.
- The coral POPULAR ribbon is threaded through the tier-3 tag's punched hole and trails to one side.
- Leave a little honest empty board visible between and around the tags. The dark negative space is a
  feature of this direction, not wasted room — but keep every tag fully on-canvas and never let a tag
  overlap another tag's numerals.
