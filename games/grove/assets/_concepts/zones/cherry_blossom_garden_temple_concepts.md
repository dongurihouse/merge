# Cherry Blossom Garden: temple concepts

Three open-content variations for the existing paper-cut Cherry Blossom Garden visual language:

- `cherry_blossom_garden_temple_concept_v1.png` — purification approach: temizuya, bamboo-fed tsukubai, koi pond, bridge, and prayer hall.
- `cherry_blossom_garden_temple_concept_v2.png` — bell-and-prayer courtyard: bell pavilion, ema rack, water rill, waterwheel, and rest garden.
- `cherry_blossom_garden_temple_concept_v3.png` — pilgrim water route: fox-guarded torii, shishi-odoshi basin, bridge, tea pavilion, and moon pond.

The content language draws on common Japanese temple-site garden motifs such as a temizuya/chōzuya purification basin, bamboo ladles or water spouts, stone lanterns, bell pavilions, ema racks, and stepping-stone water routes. These are imaginative game concepts, not recreations of named sites.
