Generate a 12-tier merge-line sheet for the Cherry-Blossom Tea Cups line.
Use the Meadow Sky + Cut-Paper Playground style block from docs/design/art-style-guide.md section 2.
Canvas: exact 1024x1536 PNG, 3 columns x 4 rows, generous gutters. Background: solid flat #FF00FF only. No visible grid.

V3 diversity rule: every tier must differ from adjacent and prior tiers by at least two of these axes: vessel silhouette, construction features, dominant color family, and broad motif/material. Do not make the same rounded handled cup with different decoration. Vary handle/no-handle, lid/no-lid, bowl/tall glass/tumbler/chalice, saucer/foot/pedestal, rim shape, and palette.

Constraints: same visual footprint across all cells; one connected tea vessel per cell; if a lid or saucer appears it must touch and visually fuse with the cup as one cutout object. No teapots, kettles, spoons, steam, liquid splashes, loose tea leaves, scenes, tables, shadows, grounding, glow, sparkles, particles, aura, smoke, text, labels, or watermark. Keep details broad and attached.
