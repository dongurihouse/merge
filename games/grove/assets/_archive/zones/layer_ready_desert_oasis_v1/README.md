# Layer-ready Desert Oasis concepts, v1

These are full composite reference mocks generated for a three-layer runtime plan:

1. **Foundation:** fixed sand, path, pond, distant dunes, and mountains.
2. **Hero-object layer:** exactly five independent replacement sockets.
3. **Cover layer:** static palms, cactus/agaves, rock clusters, and flowers placed mainly at
   the scene perimeter and socket seams.

Sky and clouds are included in the references only. They are intentionally outside the proposed
three scene layers and would become a separate atmospheric runtime layer.

| Concept | Five hero sockets |
| --- | --- |
| Market Courtyard | caravanserai gatehouse, spice stall, textile stall, tea canopy, camel cart |
| Shrine Oasis | domed pavilion, ruined arch, pond-edge tiled kiosk, carpet canopy, cargo wagon |
| Watchtower Caravan Stop | watchtower, trading house, market stall, tent, camel supply cart |

These are choice-stage concepts only: no extraction, workbench placement, or game-facing asset
has been made from them.
